💡 Key Insights

Q-table Override driving SERVICE_CRASH improvement

SERVICE_CRASH accuracy jumped from 37.5% → 100% over 5 loops. By Loop 3, the Q-table accumulated conclusive evidence (≥5 attempts, ≥80% success) for restart_service and bypassed the LLM entirely. 48 override fires total.

LLM hit rate dropped 94% → 58% — by design

As the Q-table builds confidence, the agent relies less on the LLM and more on learned evidence. This is the recursive learning loop working correctly: exploration converges to exploitation.

DEPENDENCY_FAILURE stuck at 0%

qwen2.5 strongly prefers investigate or restart_service for dependency failures. The correct action rollback is never selected, so no positive evidence accumulates and override never fires.

MTTR improved 23% — resolving incidents faster

Mean steps-to-resolution fell from 2.91 → 2.24. As the Q-table takes over from the LLM for well-understood states, it selects the correct action on the first step more often (first-attempt rate: 28.6% → 40.5% in L5).
